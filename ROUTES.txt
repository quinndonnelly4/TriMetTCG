Source: TriMet GTFS  https://developer.trimet.org/schedule/gtfs.zip
Pulled: 2026-09-29  (feed service window listed as 2026-09-13 to 2027-02-27)

What this is for
----------------
Ride log percentage by type = unique lines ridden / unique lines that exist
for that type. Check-in uses the Vehicles feed, so a line only counts if
TriMet is reporting vehicles on it that day. The lists below are the
scheduled catalog (good denominators). Disruption shuttle buses (287–293)
are listed separately so you can decide whether they belong in “bus.”

The Portland Aerial Tram is in GTFS but is not TriMet rail/bus; the app
does not treat it as a ride mode today.
